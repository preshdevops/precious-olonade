## Users

## Product Purpose

Position Precious Oluwasegun Olonade as a Computer Scientist and independent product builder whose work bridges user psychology, product architecture, and rigorous engineering. The site exists to showcase the depth of thinking behind what he builds, why it exists, and how technical craft brings it to life.

## Positioning

An editorial product studio portfolio that rejects generic developer portfolio tropes. It leads with problem statements, architectural tradeoffs, product notes, and editorial clarity rather than syntax laundry lists or decorative animations.

## Operating Context

Evaluated across modern desktop and mobile browsers by technical and product evaluators who value clarity, typography, systems thinking, and authentic craft.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with the Problem**: Never describe code without first explaining what broke, why it matters, and who hurts without it.
2. **Restraint is Confidence**: Generous margins, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and neutral materials convey authority better than decorative embellishments.
3. **Architecture is Product Design**: Security models, data schemas, and API contracts directly determine user trust and privacy.
4. **Human & Authentic**: Speak like a reflective builder actively studying the craft, not an anonymous agency or corporate entity.
