# Design System: Editorial Product Studio

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

A bespoke editorial product studio monograph. It fuses the typographic authority of an independent design review, the architectural rigor of a high-end systems whitepaper, and the intimate clarity of a thoughtful founder's notebook. It rejects the generic SaaS landing page and junior developer tropes in favor of generous whitespace, editorial typography, asymmetric structural rhythm, and hairline geometric rules.

## Palette

- **Background Deep Ink**: `#0B0C0E` — rich, deep carbon canvas
- **Surface Elevation Dark**: `#13151A` — elevated editorial containers
- **Surface Elevation Subtle**: `#1A1D24` — secondary metadata and interactive cards
- **Border Hairline**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` — crisp 1px structural rules
- **Border Active**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` — hover and active boundary states
- **Foreground Primary**: `#F4F4F6` — off-white, high-contrast headline and body text
- **Foreground Secondary**: `#A2A6B0` — neutral stone for captions, metadata, and body narratives
- **Foreground Muted**: `#6E7380` — quiet timestamps, labels, and footnote markers
- **Accent Highlight**: `#FFFFFF` — stark, pure white emphasis

*Zero neon, zero glowing gradients, zero vibe-code purple, zero colored left-border cards.*

## Typography

- **Display / Heading**: `Newsreader` / `Playfair Display` (serif, italic & roman) — expressive editorial voice for headlines, pull-quotes, and case study titles.
- **Body / Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Outfit` — clean, modern, highly legible sans-serif for narrative text and UI controls.
- **Technical & Metadata**: `JetBrains Mono` — for architectural specs, timestamps, data properties, and version stamps.

## Spacing & Rhythm

- Baseline spacing scale: 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 96px, 128px.
- Reading measure: strict 65–72ch line length for essays and case study narratives.
- Section dividers: clean, minimal 1px border lines with ample vertical breathing room (py-16 to py-28).

## Components & Anatomy

1. **Editorial Masthead (Navbar)**:
   - Minimal studio navigation: `Work`, `Thinking`, `About`, `Now`.
   - Live availability signal and time zone indicator (`Osun, NG`).
2. **Hero Monograph**:
   - Bold, editorial opening statement: "Precious Oluwasegun Olonade"
   - Mission statement: "Computer Scientist building products that solve real-world problems."
   - Clear architectural thesis without animated clutter.
3. **Product Case Studies ("Selected Work")**:
   - Featured flagship products: **Privora**, **Makarios**, **Dabar**.
   - Structured product anatomy: Problem Statement, Product Concept, Why It Was Built, Architectural Decisions, Technical Stack, and Interactive Deep-Dive View.
4. **Field Notes ("Product Thinking")**:
   - Thoughtful essays on privacy, mobile complexity, developer timing, and community technology.
   - Inline full-article drawer/expander for deep reading.
5. **Biography & Philosophy ("About")**:
   - Computer Science graduate from Osun State University.
   - Systems, privacy, mobile, and Tech4Dev focus.
6. **Live Radar ("Now")**:
   - Clean, modular status blocks highlighting active explorations in Kotlin, Rust, and new product experiments.
7. **Colophon & Footer**:
   - Real contact endpoints (Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Writing), live Spotify player, and copyright.
